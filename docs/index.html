The game

Five Finger Fillet.
On your keyboard.

Bet your own fingers in a horror roguelite that plays like Balatro meets Five Finger Fillet.

  1. 01

    Place four fingers

    Place four fingers on the keyboard. Those fingers are your life points. Lose them all and you'll bleed to death.

  2. 02

    Stab the knife

    Carefully but quickly, keep stabbing the knife between your fingers while the spinning blade closes in.

  3. 03

    Build combos

    Collect rings and Stab Items, build combos, and reach the target score before you bleed out.

The story

Five fingers.
Five bosses.

Invited to a dangerous game, you find your arm restrained. A mysterious woman controlling finger puppets forces you to play a game with your life at stake. Survive the spinning blade, defeat her, and escape.

Defeat the maniacal finger puppets one by one and gradually uncover the darkness deep within the woman controlling them.

  • 01

    Place your fingers on the keyboard and stab the knife between them. Immersion so intense, you might just lose a finger yourself.

  • 02

    The tension of the spinning blade closing in

  • 03

    The satisfying rush of chaining combos with rings and items

  • 04

    A different experience every run, with varied builds and endless synergies

  • 05

    A cast of eerie, twisted characters that are a little cute

  • 06

    An eerie death-game atmosphere
