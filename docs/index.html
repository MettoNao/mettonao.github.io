The game

Five Finger Fillet.
On your keyboard.

FFFF: Fatal Five Finger Fillet is a first-person roguelite that turns Five Finger Fillet into a physical keyboard game.

  1. 01

    Place four fingers

    Place four real fingers directly on the keyboard, where they correspond to the fingers of the in-game hand.

  2. 02

    Move the knife

    Control an on-screen knife between your fingers, balancing speed and accuracy as the pressure builds.

  3. 03

    Build the score

    Combine rings and items to change how you chase higher scores while a rotating blade closes in.
