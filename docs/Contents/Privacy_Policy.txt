# Privacy Policy — FFFF : Fatal Five Finger Fillet

**Last Updated: August 13, 2026**

Genman Games provides *FFFF : Fatal Five Finger Fillet* (the “Game”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game through Steam.

The Game does not require you to create an account with Genman Games.

We do not operate game servers for the Game and do not independently collect or store personal information, gameplay analytics, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or usage telemetry through the Game.

## 2. Steam and Steam Cloud

The Game supports Steam Cloud.

If Steam Cloud synchronization is enabled in your Steam client, save files designated for synchronization may be automatically uploaded to Steam's cloud storage and downloaded to your devices. This allows your saved game data to be backed up and synchronized between supported devices.

Steam Cloud is a service provided by Valve Corporation (“Valve”). The processing and storage of information by Steam, including Steam Cloud data and information associated with your Steam account, is governed by Valve's Steam Privacy Policy and other applicable Steam terms.

Genman Games does not operate Steam Cloud and does not maintain a separate copy of your Steam Cloud save files on servers operated by Genman Games. We do not access or retrieve your Steam Cloud save files outside the normal operation of the Game.

Steamworks may make certain Steam account-related information available to game developers, including information concerning ownership of the Game and information you choose to make public through your Steam profile. Genman Games does not use such information for advertising, profiling, or analytics, and does not independently store such information for those purposes.

You may disable Steam Cloud synchronization through the settings provided by Steam.
