## TAGLINE
Balatro meets Five Finger Fillet in a horror roguelite. Place your fingers on the keyboard and stab the knife between them.

## SHORT DESCRIPTION
Bet your own fingers in a horror roguelite that plays like Balatro meets Five Finger Fillet. Place your own fingers on the keyboard. Your fingers are your life points. Carefully but quickly, keep stabbing the knife between your fingers. Collect rings and Stab Items, build combos, and reach the target score before you bleed out.

## LONG DESCRIPTION
Bet your own fingers. FFFF is a horror roguelite that plays like Balatro meets Five Finger Fillet. Invited to a dangerous game, you find your arm restrained. A mysterious woman controlling finger puppets forces you to play a game with your life at stake. Survive the spinning blade, defeat her, and escape.
Place four fingers on the keyboard. Those fingers are your life points. Lose them all and you'll bleed to death. Game over. Carefully but quickly, keep stabbing the knife between your fingers. Collect rings and Stab Items, choose your combinations strategically, and build powerful synergies as you chase a high score.
Five fingers. Five bosses. Defeat the maniacal finger puppets one by one and gradually uncover the darkness deep within the woman controlling them.
